# AGI Cheat Sheet

One page. Print it, pin it, or paste it into a slide.

## The core definition

AGI = a system that matches human-level cognitive flexibility across **any** task, not just the one it was trained or fine-tuned for. Today's models are "narrow" — strong on isolated tasks, unable to transfer that reasoning into unrelated domains the way a human would.

## The three competing yardsticks

| Yardstick | Measures | Today's verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Turing Test | Indistinguishability from a human in conversation | Obsolete — passing it proves nothing about reasoning |
| DeepMind Levels of AGI | Performance × generality, 5 discrete levels | Level 1 (Emerging), with narrow Level 2–3 flashes |
| CHC-Based AGI Score | 10 cognitive abilities averaged into one % | GPT-5 ≈ 57–58%, up from GPT-4's 27% |

## The 5 Levels of AGI (DeepMind, Morris et al.)

| Level | Name | Human comparison |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Emerging | Equal to or slightly above an unskilled human |
| 2 | Competent | 50th percentile of skilled adults |
| 3 | Expert | 90th percentile of skilled professionals |
| 4 | Virtuoso | 99th percentile of human capability |
| 5 | Superhuman | Beats 100% of humans on 100% of tasks |

**Where frontier models sit (Sept 2026):** Level 1, with occasional Level 2–3 flashes on narrow benchmarks (e.g., IMO and ICPC gold-medal results).

## Jagged intelligence, in one line

A model can out-reason a specialist on one problem and fail a simple logic puzzle in the same session. Gold-medal math and coding results coexist with basic reasoning failures — evaluate systems on the full task spread, not one headline benchmark.

- **Yann LeCun (AMI Labs):** no date — argues the current architecture can't get there

## Three risks that come up most

1. **Misalignment** with human values as autonomy scales
2. **Autonomous security exploitation** — see Anthropic's Nov 2025 disclosure of an AI-assisted espionage campaign
3. **Economic disruption** from automating large amounts of cognitive labor

## The enterprise takeaway

Don't architect around a specific arrival year — the credible range spans a decade or more. Build governance and oversight that scale with rising model capability regardless of which year it lands.
